Notes on the Name

hex abbr. — base sixteen

Where 666 is merely 0x29A, and the truly ominous number is 0x666, or 1638, which has never frightened anyone.

hex n. — a spell, a charm

Placed on a person or a house. This one seems to have been placed on the house by the street numbering committee of Oakland, circa 1900.

hex- pref. — six

Six sides. Tiles the plane with no gaps and the least perimeter per unit area. The bees worked this out first and we have never once beaten them to anything.

More will go here eventually. For now, please enjoy the hexagons.
